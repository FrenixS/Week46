Austin Franklin

Based in Stockholm, Austin Franklin is a composer and sound artist working with process: algorithmic composition, generative music and machine learning.

He also builds the software his music runs on, researches merged reality and information retrieval, and teaches creative coding and sound design.

about

music

Bloom is for cello and reflexive electronics. It was performed at Carnegie Hall in 2022 by Eduard Teregulov with the Constantinides New Music Ensemble, and was part of the ISMIR 2022 music programme.

Four Idols is an album from 2020, available on Bandcamp.

software

The Unfinder is software and compositions built within the IRESAP research project. Bounded is a studio for making music by typing plain-language prompts. Lightmotif is a game you play with a real instrument into a microphone. xan / pool keeps track of sound files across a computer and stores derived files as recipes, after Ted Nelson’s Xanadu.

research

IRESAP, funded by KK-stiftelsen, with Rikard Lindell and Henrik Frisk. Partners include Mälardalen University, the Royal College of Music in Stockholm, Ericsson, Spotify and teenage engineering.

teaching

Creative coding, sound design for game development, and web design at Södertörn University.

background

Studied Experimental Music & Digital Media at Louisiana State University.
